# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday riders who want a faster way to check direct Riviera Trasporti buses without manually scanning the official timetable PDF. They may know the locality but not the exact stop name, and they need a calm, low-friction interface that helps them move from broad search to precise confirmation. They may also want a broader Riviera travel companion feel, not a narrow timetable utility.

## Product Purpose

`Azzuriva` turns the official Riviera Trasporti PDF into a route-first lookup tool for direct rides. Its primary job is to help riders find the fastest valid direct-route answer from an origin area to a destination stop, then confirm timing details against the official source with minimal effort and ambiguity. Its secondary job is to feel like a trusted Riviera companion, with room for nearby context and fallback options while staying explicitly independent from the operator.

## Brand Personality

Trusted, premium, calm.

The interface should feel clear-headed and useful rather than promotional. It should reduce the cognitive load of timetable lookup, communicate reliability through restraint, and make the official PDF feel easier to consult instead of replacing it with a flashy abstraction. It should feel independent and editorial enough to broaden into discovery and sponsorship later.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like an old website or a dated public-service portal. Avoid cluttered layouts, weak hierarchy, low-contrast controls, cramped form fields, legacy gradients, heavy chrome, and any interaction pattern that feels bureaucratic or visually stale.

## Design Principles

1. Search should start broad and resolve to certainty quickly.
2. Official data should feel easier to consult, not hidden behind novelty.
3. Visual calm is a usability feature: hierarchy, spacing, and copy should lower effort at every step.
4. Trust comes from legibility, consistency, and predictable interaction states.
5. Accessibility is a first-order product quality requirement, not a final polish step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for the strongest practical accessibility baseline across the product. Treat WCAG AA as the minimum standard and push key reading and form surfaces toward AAA contrast where feasible. Support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, reduced cognitive load, strong text legibility, descriptive helper copy, and motion restraint or reduced-motion-friendly behavior by default.
